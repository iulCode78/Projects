Study Buddy

HTML CSS React Firebase

Overview

Application that students can utilize for their studies. They can upload their notes, view said notes, make use of a study timer or a break timer, to time their sessions and breaks. They have the ability to listen to music through a Spotify link displayed on the application, and can make note of what tasks they must finish with the Todo-List.

Features

  • Notes : Capable of uploading and viewing notes.
  • Music : Link to Spotify list to help students with their studies.
  • Todo-List : Students can add, cross-off, or delete tasks from their list.
  • Timers : Two timers, one to time study sessions, and another to time breaks.
  • Calender : Allows users to view deadlines or certain events.
