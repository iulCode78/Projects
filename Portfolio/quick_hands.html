SHA-256

Processing

Overview

Aim of the game is for the player to prevent from the balls being scored into the goal. The player has 3 lives, if they lose all lives, the game is over.

Features

  • Player with the ability to move left and right.
  • Scoreboard for the player to showcase the points culminated from saving goals.
  • Balls that spawn at the top of the screen and descend towards the goal.
